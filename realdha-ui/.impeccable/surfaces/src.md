# Realdha app (all screens)

Mode: Operate. Build path: code-led (app UI).
Audience and job: property owners first (golden path GP-01 to GP-23), then buyers, developers, channel partners, lawyers, admins and government viewers (F01–F20, X). Each screen lets someone complete one task on a property record.
Constraints: match the "Realdha UI frames" artifact frames (1440/834/390) and their states; copy is verbatim; the DEMO bar and Sample data label are on every screen; sample data only.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A land registry made modern. Every screen reads like an official record you can trust and act on. It refuses the proptech-portal look of glossy photo heroes, gradients and crypto chrome.
OWN-WORLD: Registry blue (#1B3A6B) on cool greys (#F5F6F8), white cards with #D8DDE4 hairlines, IBM Plex Sans with Noto Sans Devanagari, and Plex Mono only for IDs and hashes. Seal teal is reserved for on-chain facts. Every status shows an icon, a word and a colour. Dark mode is Graphite (#111316 / #1A1D21) with a solid blue primary.
STORY: The owner sees exactly where their property stands (verdict, open findings, sign-off, anchoring), understands why from cited document pages, and takes the next step.
FIRST VIEWPORT: As drawn in each frame of the artifact. Its primary action sits where the frame puts it.
FORM: Pinned whole by the user ("build all of the UI screens, flows exactly as the artifact attached"). seed: none (pinned).
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
